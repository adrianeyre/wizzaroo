# Wizzaroo

> **Personal use only.** Wizzaroo is intended for your own personal use only. It is not intended for use with or by a school, teacher, parent or student. Only use it for yourself.

Wizzaroo is a 3D learning game for primary-school children that teaches the UK National Curriculum. Children progress by learning, and they spend what they earn on their own Character.

## People and accounts

**School**:
A real primary school that Wizzaroo serves. Every Child attends exactly one, and its Teachers see the Children in their Year Groups there. Only an Admin creates one.
_Avoid_: Organisation, tenant, academy

**Teacher**:
An adult at one School who teaches one or more Year Groups there. A Teacher sees the progress of every Child in those Year Groups, and creates and invites Parents. The only thing a Teacher changes about how a Child plays is a Teacher Lock. Only an Admin creates and invites one.
_Avoid_: Staff, educator, tutor

**Teacher Lock**:
An Island, Region or Game a Child's Teacher has turned off for them. It stays off whatever their Parents' own switches say, and only a Teacher or the Admin turns it back on.
_Avoid_: Restriction, block, override

**Family**:
The household a Child plays in: its Parents, its Children and what they share, such as the Family Chest, Gifts and Family Devices. Siblings in one Family may attend different Schools.
_Avoid_: Household, tenant, account

**Parent**:
An adult in a Family who manages its Children and sees their progress. A Family has one or more Parents, all with equal rights. A Parent belongs to every School one of their Children attends, and to the School that invited them. Only an Admin or a Teacher creates and invites one.
_Avoid_: Guardian, owner, user

**Invite**:
A single-use link an Admin or a Teacher creates so an adult can set up their sign-in as a Teacher or a Parent. It expires after seven days. Parents never create Invites, and no one can sign up without one.
_Avoid_: Sign-up, registration, referral

**Admin**:
The role that runs Wizzaroo itself: Schools, Teachers, accounts, content and the Catalogue. It sees every School, Teacher, Parent and Child. Only the owner holds it, together with Parent.
_Avoid_: Superuser, staff

**Deletion Request**:
A Parent's request for one of their Children, or their whole Family, to be removed from Wizzaroo. Nothing is deleted until the Admin confirms it; then every row about them goes, including what they played.
_Avoid_: Erasure request, takedown

**Audit Log**:
The record of security actions only the Admin reads: sign-ins, sign-in failures, credential changes, and people, Schools and Families created or deleted. Each entry names ids, never names, and is kept for twelve months.
_Avoid_: Activity log, history, access log

**Child**:
A young learner in a Family, attending one School, who only ever plays and never holds an email address or password. Only their birth month and year are kept, never the day.
_Avoid_: Kid, student, pupil, player

**Family Device**:
A tablet or computer a Parent has signed in on, so the Family's Children can play on it. Children log in there with a picture password.
_Avoid_: Trusted device, kiosk, child device

**Sign-in Code**:
A short code an adult makes on a device where they're signed in, and types on another device to sign in there, such as a tablet that can't use their passkey. It works once, for 10 minutes.
_Avoid_: Pairing code, device code, login link

**Classmate**:
Another Child at the same School in the same Year Group, from another Family. A Child can visit a Classmate's Room only if both their Parents have turned it on, and sees the Room and the Character's portrait, never a name.
_Avoid_: Friend, classfellow, peer

**Picture Password**:
The short sequence of pictures a Child taps to open their Character on a Family Device. It keeps siblings apart; it is not a security boundary.
_Avoid_: PIN, child password

## The Character

**Character**:
The 3D person each Child owns, names, makes and dresses up. The picture a Child taps to log in is their Character's portrait.
_Avoid_: Avatar, Wizzaroo (that is the product), pet

**Character Type**:
Who a Character is, such as a Wizard, a Warrior or an Astronaut. Each brings its own gear (a hat, an outfit, something on its back and something in its hand), worn wherever the Child hasn't chosen an Item instead. Choosing one is free.
_Avoid_: Class, role, job

**Feature**:
A part of how a Character looks that a Child chooses for free, at any time: the Character Type, skin, hair style and colour, eyes and eye colour, eyebrows, mouth and facial hair. Features are never earned or bought.
_Avoid_: Customisation, body part

**Item**:
An earnable reward from the Catalogue, such as a hat, a Pet or a piece of Furniture. Never a question.
_Avoid_: Reward, unlockable, cosmetic

**Catalogue**:
Every Item that can be earned, with its rarity, its unlock rule and its Star cost.
_Avoid_: Shop inventory, store

**Gift**:
An Item a Child buys only to give to a sibling. It can never be kept.
_Avoid_: Present, transfer, trade

**Room**:
Each Child's own space, opened from the World Map and never placed on an Island. A Child furnishes it and keeps their Trophies there.
_Avoid_: House, bedroom, den, base

**Furniture**:
An Item for one fixed place in a Room, such as the bed, the rug or the lamp. Every place has free starter choices, so a Room is never empty.
_Avoid_: Decoration, prop

**Trophy**:
A mark of effort shown in a Child's Room, awarded once the Child has earned enough Stars in one Region to unlock every Game there themselves, whatever a Parent has unlocked, or once they solve every Puzzle of an Island's Quest. The Brainwave Big Top gives none. A Trophy is never bought and is not an Item.
_Avoid_: Badge, achievement. A Quest's Trophy is called an Award only in the Awards window. The Parent page's **Awards** page is a Child's Stars, the Family Chest and their Quests, not their Trophies.

**Pet**:
An Item that stands beside a Child's Character wherever the Character is shown. It is not the Companion, and it never appears on the World Map.
_Avoid_: Sidekick, Companion

**Mount**:
An Item a Child's Character rides, such as a broomstick, a cloud or a dragon.
_Avoid_: Vehicle, ride

**Vehicle**:
Something a Child buys with Stars in the Vehicles part of the wardrobe and rides when exploring an Island, such as a bicycle, a horse and cart or a sports car. It isn't worn, so it isn't in the Character's loadout: the Child puts one on the map beside the Character and gets on and off it.
_Avoid_: Car (for all of them), kart, ride

**Balloon Field**:
The round, level landing place on every Island where the hot-air balloon comes down to pick the Character up and to set them down, marked with a landing cross. The Portal opens on it too, and the dragon lands on it.
_Avoid_: Landing pad, launch site

**Portal**:
The magic ring of stone that opens on an Island's Balloon Field for the Character to walk through, one of the fifteen ways of travelling between Islands. It closes to a wisp of light that crosses the World Map and opens again on the other Island's Balloon Field. It is not a craft and nothing is ridden.
_Avoid_: Teleporter, gate. A tunnel's opening is a tunnel portal, never the Portal.

**Dive Locker**:
The Wizzaroo cabinet that grows up on the Dock's pier when the Character goes scuba diving to another Island. The Character takes the scuba gear from it before jumping in, and hangs the gear up in the one that grows on the other Island's pier. It is not a craft and nothing is ridden.
_Avoid_: Kit box, scuba station

**Railway**:
The loop of track that runs round every land mass of an Island, crossing the sea and the rivers on bridges of several styles and going through hills in tunnels. Each Island has its own Train on it.
_Avoid_: Line, track (for the whole loop)

**Station**:
One of the five stops on an Island's Railway, with a platform and a station building, where its Train waits so the Character can get on or off.
_Avoid_: Stop, halt (except in a Station's own name)

**Train**:
The locomotive and carriages that go round an Island's Railway, stopping at each Station. Every Island's Train is a different style; Meadow Isle's is a steam train.
_Avoid_: Engine (for the whole Train)

**Attraction**:
A place to visit on an Island's new lands, such as a playground, a bakery, a farm or a castle. Each Island has twenty or twenty-one: five or six dealt out at random, with a school on every Island, and fifteen more of its own, such as Meadow Isle's swan boat lake or Starfall Isle's drop tower, out on the far reaches of its two new lands, seven or eight on each. Each is kept in the same place. Its sign tells the Child a fact, many let the Character have a go on them, and each of the fifteen also has a button on its card, as a Curiosity has.
_Avoid_: Item (Items are what the Character wears), building, landmark (a Region's Landmark)

**Far reach**:
The outer end of each of an Island's two new lands, beyond its old coast, joined to it by a trail and a bridge, with a loop trail round it. The fifteen Attractions of the Island's own stand there.
_Avoid_: Island, new land (the far reach is part of one)

**Curiosity**:
A small thing to find and play with anywhere on an Island, such as a windmill, a wishing well or a penguin colony. Each Island has twenty of its own, spread out over the whole Island, and each teaches a fact and does something as the Character comes near. It is smaller than an Attraction.
_Avoid_: Item (Items are what the Character wears), Attraction, Wonder, prop

**Quest**:
An Island's own adventure of thirty Puzzles, with a story, such as Meadow Isle's Great Meadow Fair. Finishing it gives a Trophy and Stars. Each Child's progress is worked out from their play events, so it carries on from where they got to.
_Avoid_: Mission (a Mission is a Game's Activities), adventure (the Island's trails, signs and treasures), level

**Find**:
Something a Child picks up on an Island, or is given by a Puzzle, and keeps in their rucksack until it is used, such as a rusty key or a jar of honey. Each Quest has thirty, each with a 3D picture in the rucksack.
_Avoid_: Item (Items are what the Character wears), treasure (treasures are the gems collected while exploring), inventory

**Islander**:
One of the ten people on each Island's Quest, such as Officer Patel the Police Officer, whom the Child talks to for hints and gives Finds to.
_Avoid_: Friend (friends are the Island's animals), NPC, actor, character (the Character is the Child's own)

**Quest Puzzle**:
One step of a Quest: one Find used on one Islander or thing, which may give another Find. The last Puzzle of each line asks a question from the National Curriculum.
_Avoid_: Puzzle on its own (the Brainwave Big Top's Games are puzzles), task, challenge

**Emote**:
An Item that is a short dance, wave or cheer the Character does when a Mission ends or when the Child taps them.
_Avoid_: Dance, gesture, animation

**Spell Effect**:
An Item that is a trail or sparkle around the Character, shown when a Mission ends or when the Child taps them.
_Avoid_: Particle, aura, magic

**Star**:
The only currency in Wizzaroo, earned through effort and spent on Items. Stars a Child earns in a Region also unlock that Region's next Game, without being spent. The Admin can also give a Child Stars, and so can their Parents from the Awards page, 5, 10 or 15 at a time and at most 15 a day; those are only spent, and unlock no Game.
_Avoid_: Coin, point, score

## Curriculum

**Subject**:
A curriculum area of the National Curriculum in England: Maths, Phonics, English, Science, Music, Geography, History, Computing, Art and design, Design and technology or Languages (French, from Year 3).
_Avoid_: Topic, course

**Skill**:
One thing a Child can master, such as number bonds to 10. Each has a Subject, a Year Group, prerequisites and a mastery rule.
_Avoid_: Topic, objective, lesson

**Year Group**:
A school year from Year 1 to Year 6. It belongs to a Child as their real school year, worked out from their birth month and year so it moves up every 1 September, with an optional one-year adjustment for a Child who is not in their age's year, to a Skill as the year the curriculum places it in, and to an Island as the year it teaches. A Child's Year Group decides their home Island.
_Avoid_: Level, grade, age

**Phonics Programme**:
A school's systematic synthetic phonics scheme, such as Little Wandle or Read Write Inc, as the order it teaches sounds in and the words it uses for them. Chosen per Child.
_Avoid_: Phonics scheme, phonics course

**Curriculum Point**:
A key point of the National Curriculum for one Subject in one Year Group, such as Year 2 Statistics, taught by one or more Skills. A Parent sees how far a Child is through each one. Each Subject's points link to its programme of study on GOV.UK.
_Avoid_: Objective, bullet point, standard, outcome

**For parents**:
What a Game practises in the National Curriculum in England, told for a grown-up: the programme of study, Key Stage, Year and statutory heading of each Curriculum Point its Skills teach. Opened from the small button on a Game's card, or from the button while playing, which pauses the Game.
_Avoid_: Info, help, about this game

**Gap**:
A Curriculum Point a Child hasn't started, or one whose Skills they are getting wrong more often than a good day allows.
_Avoid_: Weakness, problem area

**Mastery**:
The state of one Child on one Skill: Not started, Learning, Mastered, or Due for review. A Skill becomes Mastered when the Child meets its mastery rule.
_Avoid_: Completion, progress, score

**Review**:
An Activity on a Mastered Skill that spaced repetition has scheduled because the Skill is Due for review.
_Avoid_: Revision, recap, practice

**Difficulty**:
How hard a generator makes the Questions it produces for a Skill.
_Avoid_: Level

## Play

**Mission**:
A short stretch of play, 5 to 10 minutes long, with a clear end and a celebration. Made up of Activities.
_Avoid_: Level, lesson, quest, round

**Activity**:
One Game Type played on one Skill, inside a Mission.
_Avoid_: Exercise, task

**Game Type**:
A reusable kind of game, such as matching pairs or building words from tiles, that works across Subjects. A Maths Game Type plays a different Skill for each Year Group, so the same scene grows with the Child.
_Avoid_: Mini-game, template

**Game**:
One of the fixed, named things to play in a Region on one Island, such as one of a Region's ten games for that Island's Year Group. A Game starts locked or unlocked, and is the unit that Stars unlock one at a time.
_Avoid_: Level, Activity, Mission

**Science Game**:
A Game in a Science Region, each teaching its own Skill from that Island's Year of the Science programme of study.
_Avoid_: Experiment, quiz

**Lab Station**:
How a lab game asks its Questions, such as sorting into groups, finding the odd one out or predicting what happens. Each Year Group's ten Science Games use the ten Lab Stations once each, and each Lab Station has its own 3D choices. Lab games in other Subjects use only the six Lab Stations whose spoken instruction names no Subject.

**Lab game**:
A multiple-choice Game written as data and played in the lab, in any Subject. Each teaches named statutory requirements of the National Curriculum and is its own Skill unless it plays one that already exists. Lab games fill the gaps other Games leave, as extra Games, and are every Game in the Art and design, Design and technology and Languages Regions.
_Avoid_: Mechanic, mode, round

**Games first**:
A Parent's limit of minutes of Games a Child plays each day, on each Family Device, before the Wardrobe, the Room and Explore open. Only time in a Game counts. Not set by default.
_Avoid_: Screen time, homework time, learning time

**Question**:
One prompt and the answer a Child gives to it, inside an Activity.
_Avoid_: Item, problem, card

**World Map**:
The sea holding all six Islands, which a Child can move around freely, seeing which Islands, Regions and Games they have unlocked.
_Avoid_: Level select, overworld

**Island**:
The land for one Year Group, with its own look, landmarks and Regions, and with Games teaching that Year alone. A Child starts on their home Island and can travel to and explore every Island, whatever their Year Group. The Regions on the Islands for their own Year Group and below are open to play; those above stay locked until a Parent opens them early, and a Parent or their Teacher can lock any Island.
_Avoid_: World, level, Region

**Home Island**:
The Island for a Child's own Year Group, which the World Map opens on, zoomed out to show the whole Island.
_Avoid_: Start island, current island

**Region**:
A themed area on one Island holding the Games for one Subject (or, for the Brainwave Big Top, puzzles, and for the Challenge Arena, Checks). Each Island has a Region for every Subject taught in its Year, with names and landmarks of its own, plus the Brainwave Big Top, and the Challenge Arena on the Islands whose Year has a statutory check. Every Region is on by default with only its first Game unlocked, except the Brainwave Big Top and the Challenge Arena, whose Games are all unlocked.
_Avoid_: Island, world, zone, level

**Brainwave Big Top**:
The one Region found on every Island, under the same name: puzzles playing Maths and English Skills from that Island's Year, with all its Games unlocked from the start.
_Avoid_: Puzzle region, circus

**Challenge Arena**:
The Region, under the same name on every Island that has one, where a Child practises the statutory checks of that Island's Year: the Phonics Screening Check on Island 1, the Multiplication Tables Check on Island 4 and the SATs on Island 6. All its Games are unlocked from the start.
_Avoid_: Challenge mode, exam room, test zone

**Check**:
A Game in the Challenge Arena that practises one statutory assessment, or one paper of it, in that assessment's own format, short enough to fit a Mission. It plays the same Skills as the Subject Regions, so it counts towards the same Mastery, except a Check read aloud, such as the Phonics Screening Check, which a Parent sitting with the Child marks.
_Avoid_: Test, exam, quiz, mock

**Practice**:
Playing a Check untimed, with the usual hints and retries.
_Avoid_: Training, easy mode

**Timed Check**:
Playing a Check against the clock the real assessment uses, with no hints until the end. A Question the clock runs out on is marked "not in time" and the Check moves on; nothing is failed or lost. Its result is a score the Child sees for themselves, and goes into their Check History. A Parent can take the clock away for a Child.
_Avoid_: Exam, test run, hard mode

**Check History**:
Every Timed Check a Child has finished, with its date, score and the facts or question types to practise. Their Parents and Teachers see it; siblings and other Children never do.
_Avoid_: Results, report card, test scores

**Companion**:
The character that travels with a Child through the World Map and reacts to what they do. It is not the Child's Character.
_Avoid_: Guide, mascot, helper, pet

**Family Chest**:
A goal the Children of a Family fill together by finishing Activities, each adding a gold coin. When it is full, each of them receives an Item. The Admin can add gold coins too, each counting as one finished Activity.
_Avoid_: Team goal, shared reward, pot

**Session**:
The time from when a Child sits down to play until they stop. It is what a Parent sees as time spent.
_Avoid_: Visit, playthrough
