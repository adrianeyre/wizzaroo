## Read it to me's voices

Read it to me speaks with sixteen voices, on the device, from the model files pinned in [`src/curriculum/piper-voice-models.ts`](../../curriculum/piper-voice-models.ts) ([ADR 0062](../../../docs/architectural-decision-record/0062-read-it-to-me-speaks-with-every-piper-voice-on-the-device.md), [ADR 0074](../../../docs/architectural-decision-record/0074-sixteen-voices-mostly-from-the-uk-each-a-fantasy-character.md)): thirteen from around the UK, one from Ireland and two from the US. Four models come from [Bryce Beattie's page](https://brycebeattie.com/files/tts/), which says of them all: "Feel free to use these for any legal and ethical purpose", and lets them be hosted elsewhere. Four come from Piper's own voices, [`rhasspy/piper-voices`](https://huggingface.co/rhasspy/piper-voices/tree/e9c8a39444359388adaa94255becb37a5a2b8e51/en/en_GB) at revision `e9c8a39444359388adaa94255becb37a5a2b8e51`, each under its dataset's licence as its model card states.

Changing a voice means changing this file in the same commit, with the new voice's source and licence.
