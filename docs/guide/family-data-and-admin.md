# Your Family's data and the Admin dashboard

[← Back to the README](../../README.md) · [All guides](README.md)

> **Personal use only.** Wizzaroo is intended for your own personal use only. It is not intended for use with or by a school, teacher, parent or student. Only use it for yourself.

## Your Family's data

The dashboard's **Your Family's data** panel is where a Parent gets their Family's data and has it removed, and answers a Teacher who asks to add an adult to the Family.

- **Adults asking to join.** When a Teacher brings in a household's next adult, they don't join the Family yet: every Parent of the Family is emailed, and the panel lists each adult waiting, with their name, the address their Invite would go to and when they were asked for (`GET /api/family-join-requests`). **Let them join** (`PUT /api/family-join-requests/:userId/agreement`) makes them a Parent of the Family, emails them their Invite, and tells the Family's other Parents. **Turn it down** (`DELETE /api/family-join-requests/:userId`) removes the adult, who never had an Invite. Only a Parent of that Family sees or answers them; both answers are in the audit log.
- **Downloading it.** **Download your Family's data** saves `wizzaroo-family-data.json` from `GET /api/family-export`: one readable JSON file with a list for every table holding a Child's or an adult's data, each with that Family's rows. That is the Family, its Parents' accounts, every Child, their Character and every event they played, the Family Devices, Invites and deletion requests, and the downloading Parent's own sign-ins, passkeys, codes and checks. It also holds the downloading Parent's own [audit log](security-and-backups.md#the-audit-log) entries (`auditLogEntries`), each one they took or one taken about them, with the IP address a sign-in came from. An entry someone else took about them, such as the Admin sending a reset, names that person's role but not who they were. Passwords, Picture Password hashes, two-factor secrets and every token or code hash are left out, so the file holds nothing that would let someone sign in. It never holds a row from another Family, even when the Admin downloads theirs.
- **Asking for deletion.** A Parent chooses a Child, or the whole Family, and asks for it to be deleted. That stores a Deletion Request (`deletion_requests`) and deletes nothing yet, and row-level security refuses a Parent's or a Family Device's reach any deletion of the Family, its Children or their play; asking twice for the same thing gives back the same request. The panel lists the Family's requests waiting for the Admin.
- **What deletion removes.** Once the Admin confirms, deleting a Child removes their row, their Character, every event they played, and any Gift event a sibling bought naming them, so no row about them remains. Deleting a Family removes the Family, its Children and everything about them, its Family Devices and Invites, and its Parents' accounts with their sign-ins, passkeys and codes. The Admin's own Family can't be deleted while they are its Parent. The record of each time the Admin was served the Child's data (`admin_access_logs`, below) outlives the deletion, holding the Child's bare id, until it is twelve months old, as the Audit Log's `child.deleted` entry does. Every deleted Family, Child and Parent's id, and nothing else, is kept in `confirmed_deletions`, beside the Audit Log's, so that restoring a backup taken before the deletion deletes them again rather than bringing them back. The server's log names them by bare id alone, never by name, nickname, birth date or email address, and keeps at most 30 MB per container ([the server's log](security-and-backups.md#the-servers-log)).

## The Admin dashboard

A Parent who also holds the Admin role sees **Admin** in the sidebar; nobody else does, and the `/api/admin/` routes refuse anyone without the role. It lists the Schools, the Deletion Requests waiting, each with who asked and what for, and every Family with its Children. Every deletion asks **Yes, delete … for good** before it happens. Confirming a request deletes what it names, as described in [Your Family's data](#your-familys-data). The Admin deletes only what a Parent of the Family has asked to delete, so the dashboard offers a deletion only beside a request, and never beside a Family or Child in the list. `DELETE /api/admin/children/:childId` and `DELETE /api/admin/families/:familyId` refuse with 409, "A Parent of this Family has to ask for this deletion first", unless a request for that Child, or for the whole Family, is waiting, and then confirm it exactly as **Yes, delete … for good** does. A request for the whole Family doesn't let the Admin delete one Child of it. Each time the dashboard serves the Admin a Child's data, whether a list naming the Child, a Child's progress, or a change that replies with the Child, it logs one row in `admin_access_logs` naming the Admin, the Child, when, and what the reply served them, which that Child's Family reads in its export. A `HEAD` request is answered with no body, so it serves no Child's data and logs nothing. What was served (`served`) is one value per reply path, so a progress read can be told from a list or a change: `child-progress.opened`, `school-children-progress.listed`, `families.listed`, `parents.listed`, `deletion-requests.listed`, `child.changed`, `child-limits.changed`, `child-teacher-locks.changed`, `child-classmate-visits.changed`, `child-birth.given` or `child-school.changed`. The log is append-only to the app's `wizzaroo_reach` role: it adds a row only in the signed-in Admin's own name, at the time of the reply, and never changes or removes one. `child_id` is not a foreign key, so deleting the Child, or their Family, keeps their rows, holding the Child's bare id; the Family reads them only while the Child is still theirs. The server's daily purge deletes a deleted Child's rows once they are twelve months old, the Audit Log's retention. Deleting the Admin's account keeps them too, as the [audit log](security-and-backups.md#the-audit-log) keeps its entries. The Admin's changes to another Family's Child, its Family Chest or a School are written to the audit log as well, in the Admin's name and with no values: Stars (`child.stars-awarded`), Family Chest coins (`family-chest.coins-awarded`), a School's name or town (`school.changed`), and a Child's details, limits, Teacher locks, Classmate visits and birth (`child.changed`, `child.limits-changed`, `child.teacher-locks-changed`, `child.classmate-visits-changed`, `child.birth-given`).

- **The second-School gate.** Once one School exists, `POST /api/schools` refuses another, from the Admin dashboard's **Schools** or the API, until the server's `SECOND_SCHOOL_GATE_MET` is `true`. The refusal names everything the gate needs: legal advice, ICO registration, a privacy policy and a data processing agreement with each School, row-level security, data export and deletion, logged Admin access, off-site backups, a penetration test with every finding above low remediated or waived in writing, a second round by someone outside the project, and a request-rate policy. `SECOND_SCHOOL_GATE_MET` is empty or `false` until the owner confirms all of them are in place, and the server refuses to start with any value but `true` or `false`.
