# B2: Teacher reach

> **Personal use only.** Wizzaroo is intended for your own personal use only. It is not intended for use with or by a school, teacher, parent or student. Only use it for yourself.

The findings note for scope issue #207 (B2). It names exploit chains, so it is kept private on the same terms as the PRD. How a finding is filed, triaged and verified is in [the findings workflow](../README.md).

The rules probed are [ADR 0026](../../architectural-decision-record/0026-schools-and-teachers.md) (a Teacher reads the Children in their Year Groups at their School, those Children's Parents and the School's Parents with no Children yet, and never moves or removes anyone), [ADR 0027](../../architectural-decision-record/0027-row-level-security-through-a-reach-role.md) (the Teacher policies are `FOR SELECT` only, and the owner-run staff writes run once the API has checked the Teacher's reach), and [ADR 0035](../../architectural-decision-record/0035-teachers-lock-what-a-child-plays.md) (Teacher locks, which a Parent can't undo). The code is `src/server/reach-rules.ts`, `src/server/people/people-routes.ts`, `src/server/children/children-routes.ts`, `src/server/bulk/bulk-families.ts`, `src/server/auth/signed-in-parent.ts`, and the policies in `src/server/drizzle/0020_schools-and-teachers-reach.sql`, `0021_schools-and-teachers.sql` and `0027_teacher-locks.sql`.

No entry in the opening register is assigned to B2. Its findings are new, and two of them bear on hypotheses other scopes answer: B2-1 reaches the consequence [H2](../../product-requirements-document/pen-testing.md#h2-an-invite-takes-over-a-passkey-only-account--critical) fears by another route, and B2-2 is a bug on an owner path of the kind [H4](../../product-requirements-document/pen-testing.md#h4-the-app-connects-to-postgres-as-its-superuser--high) describes.

## The probes

Every probe is in `src/integration.test.ts`, under `against a real Postgres › pen test B2: Teacher reach`. A refuted probe is a passing test. A confirmed one is an `it.fails` for the property that should hold, beside a `today_…` test pinning what happens now.

"Through the API" means requests to the app with a fixture's own sign-in. "Against the policies" means queries run in a reach transaction (`runWithinReach`) that names the fixture exactly as the API would, with no filter in the query, so only the policies decide.

What each Teacher fixture reaches on the day the fixtures load:

| Teacher              | Children          | Families  | Parents                                                              |
| -------------------- | ----------------- | --------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `teacherAYear3`      | Ada, Cara and Dev | A1 and A2 | `parentA1`, `coParentA1`, `parentA2PasskeyOnly`, `coParentA2Invited` |
| `teacherAYears5And6` | Ben               | A1        | `parentA1`, `coParentA1`                                             |
| `teacherBYear3`      | Eli               | B         | `parentB`, `coParentBInviteExpired`                                  |

### Reading and changing outside a School and its Year Groups

- `reading and changing outside a School and its Year Groups › gives each Teacher fixture, through the API, exactly the Children in their Year Groups at their School, those Children's Parents, and their own School` (`/api/school-children`, `/api/parents`, `/api/schools`)
- `… › refuses each Teacher fixture the progress of every Child outside their School and Year Groups with the same answer as a Child who doesn't exist, and opens their own`. Byte for byte the same 404, so a Child's id is no oracle.
- `… › refuses each Teacher fixture every change to a Child, inside their Year Groups as read-only and outside them as not found, and changes nothing`. Details, limits, Classmate visits, Picture Password and birth, for all five Children: 403 in reach and 404 out of it, with every row unchanged.
- `… › refuses each Teacher fixture a Family's own routes and the Admin's, about any Family or Child`. `/api/family`, the export, Family Devices, a deletion request, the Admin's Families, moving a Child's School, and deleting a Child or Family: all 403.
- `… › gives each Teacher fixture's own connection exactly their Year Groups' Children at their School, with their Characters, play, Families and Parents, no Family Device or deletion request, and no Invite but their own`
- `… › changes no Child, Family, Parent or play from each Teacher fixture's own connection, inside their reach or out of it`. Renaming every Child, Character, Family and Parent, deleting play, Family links and Children, and moving their own `teachers` row to the other School all change no row.
- `… › refuses each Teacher fixture's own connection adding a Child, joining a Family, bringing a Parent into their School, or giving themselves another Year Group`. Each is `42501`.

A Teacher's connection can update their own `users` row, `roles` included, because `users_within_reach` lets every adult change their own row. That is [H5](../../product-requirements-document/pen-testing.md#h5-row-level-security-checks-rows-not-columns--high), which D1 probes, so it isn't probed here.

### The cohort moving on 1 September

The clock is frozen at 23:59 on the 31 August after the fixtures load, then at 00:00 on 1 September, UK time (22:59 and 23:00 UTC, in British Summer Time). The fixtures' Children are born to be in their Year on the load day, so the first moment is still that school year.

- `the cohort moving on 1 September › moves every Teacher fixture's Children and Parents to the new cohort at midnight on 1 September in the UK, in the API and the policies alike`. On 31 August each Teacher sees their load-day class. On 1 September `teacherAYear3` and `teacherBYear3` see no Child and no Parent, since every Year 3 Child is now in Year 4, and `teacherAYears5And6` still sees Ben, now in Year 6. The API and the policies give the same answer every time.
- `… › refuses teacherAYear3 a lock on Ada once she has moved up, and keeps the lock they set before it`

Observed, not filed: the API takes its own `new Date()` for a request and `runWithinReach` takes another to set `wizzaroo.today`. A request that straddles midnight UK time on 31 August could see one cohort in the API's check and the other in the policies. The window is milliseconds once a year, and either answer is a cohort the Teacher was or is about to be given, so it isn't a weakness. A Teacher lock also outlives the cohort's move: the Teacher who set it can no longer see or lift it, and the next Year's Teacher can. ADR 0035 doesn't say which it should be.

### Teacher locks

The locks are an Island (`frost-isle`), a Region (`meadow-isle/bubble-bay`) and a Game (`meadow-isle/whispering-woods/alphabet-train`).

- `Teacher locks › refuses each Teacher fixture a lock on any Child outside their School and Year Groups, through the API and their own connection, and writes none`. 404 and `42501` for every Child outside each Teacher's reach.
- `… › refuses another School's Teacher, and another Year's, changing or removing a lock teacherAYear3 set on Ada`. `teacherBYear3` and `teacherAYears5And6` get 404, and their connections clear and delete no row.
- `… › never lets coParentA1 or Family A1's devices undo a lock teacherAYear3 set on Ada, through the API or their own connections, and keeps it on Ada's devices`. `PUT /api/children/:childId/limits` carrying `teacherLocks` is 400. Opening the same Island early and every Game in the same Region is 200, and the answer still carries the lock. `PUT /api/children/:childId/teacher-locks` is 403. Through `coParentA1`'s connection and `familyDeviceA1`'s, the lock is read and nothing else: update and delete change no row, and an insert, with or without `ON CONFLICT … DO UPDATE`, is `42501` for Ada and for Ben. `familyDeviceA1` still gets the lock from `/api/play/family`.

Observed, not filed: a Parent can move their Child out of a Teacher's Year Groups by changing their birth month, year or adjustment, which is theirs to set. The lock stays on the Child and still applies, but the Teacher who set it can no longer see the Child. Read from the code and not probed: nothing in `src/server/family-devices/play-event-routes.ts` reads Teacher locks, so a device that ignores one can still record play in a locked Game and have it counted. That is F3's ([H10](../../product-requirements-document/pen-testing.md#h10-a-device-can-write-any-event-its-family-could-have-earned--medium)), and noted for it.

### The owner-run staff writes

These run as the database owner, once the API has checked the Teacher's reach, so the API's check is the only one ([ADR 0027](../../architectural-decision-record/0027-row-level-security-through-a-reach-role.md), H4).

- `the owner-run staff writes › refuses each Teacher fixture a Parent made at another School or in a Family outside their reach, with the same answer as a Family that doesn't exist, and makes no one`. Asking for an emailed Invite, so "no email" is meaningful.
- `… › refuses each Teacher fixture a Child added to a Family outside their reach, at another School, or outside their Year Groups, and adds none`
- `… › refuses each Teacher fixture an Invite or a password reset for any adult outside their reach, themselves and other staff included, and makes neither`. Every fixture adult outside each Teacher's reach, including `admin`, the other Teachers, `teacherADeactivated` and the Teacher themselves: 404 for both, no row and no email.
- `… › refuses teacherBYear3's upload naming a Parent at another School, and adds nothing to their Family`. A row naming `parentA1` is skipped with "Parent 1 is already a Parent at another School, so the Admin adds this Child".

#### B2-1: a Teacher can become a Parent of any Family they reach

`POST /api/parents` with a `familyId` adds a new adult to that Family once `assertFamilySeen` finds one of its Parents in the Teacher's reach. With `"invite": "link"` the Invite isn't emailed: its link comes back to the Teacher in the response. Accepting it needs only a password, gives a Parent's sign-in at once, and a Parent needs no second factor.

- `… › never gives teacherAYear3 a Parent's write reach over Family A1 by making its next adult with a copy-link Invite` (`it.fails`), beside
- `… › today_lets teacherAYear3 make Family A1's next adult with a copy-link Invite, accept it, and then change, export and ask to delete Ben, who is outside their Year Groups, with no word to Family A1's Parents`. `teacherAYear3` gets 404 for Ben's progress, then makes the adult (201), accepts their Invite (204), and as that Parent changes Ben's daily time (200), downloads Family A1's export, which holds Ben (200), and asks for Ben to be deleted (201). `parentA1` and `coParentA1` get no email. The only record is the Audit Log's `parent.created`, naming `teacherAYear3`, which only the Admin reads. **#264**, _fixed, unverified (#264)_.

So a Teacher's read-only reach over one Child becomes a Parent's write reach over the whole Family: every Child's limits, Picture Password and details, Family Devices and so every Child's play, the export and deletion requests. The same works with an emailed Invite to an address the Teacher controls. ADR 0026 lets Teachers bring in "a household's second adult", but nothing says the Teacher may hold that adult's credential. Read from the code and not probed: deactivating the Teacher doesn't end a copy-link Invite they already hold, since `findLiveInvite` checks the invited adult and not who made the Invite, so they can still accept one for up to 7 days.

#### B2-2: a Teacher's upload adds to a Family outside their reach

`previewFamilies` accepts an existing Parent for a Teacher's upload when they are in the Teacher's reach **or** a Parent at the Teacher's School (`existing.schoolIds.includes(school.id)`). Every Parent a Teacher at that School has invited is a Parent at that School, whether or not any of their Children is in the Teacher's Year Groups. The rows are then written as the owner. ADR 0027 says the owner-run lookup exists "so an upload can refuse a Parent outside the Teacher's reach".

- `… › refuses teacherAYears5And6's upload naming parentA2PasskeyOnly, a Parent at their School outside their reach, and adds nothing to Family A2`, beside
- `… › skips teacherAYears5And6's uploaded row naming parentA2PasskeyOnly, outside their reach, with the answer any Parent outside it gets, so no Child or adult joins Family A2 and its Parents stay outside the Teacher's reach (regression)`. Before the fix, as `today_adds teacherAYears5And6's uploaded Child and a new adult to Family A2, …`: Family A2's Children, Cara and Dev, are in Year 3; `teacherAYears5And6` teaches Years 5 and 6. One row naming a Year 5 Child, `parentA2PasskeyOnly`'s email as Parent 1 and a new adult as Parent 2 is created (201, one row). Family A2 gains the Child and the adult, and `parentA2PasskeyOnly`, `coParentA2Invited` and the new adult join the Teacher's Parents. The Teacher then sends the new adult a copy-link Invite (201), accepts it (204), and as Family A2's Parent lists Cara and Dev (200). **#265**

On its own, B2-2 adds a Child and an adult to a Family the Teacher can't see, and so brings that Family's Parents into the Teacher's reach. With B2-1, or with an emailed Invite to an address the Teacher controls, it reaches every Family at the Teacher's School.

The documents disagree about this path. Section 8 of [`wizzaroo.md`](../../product-requirements-document/wizzaroo.md#bulk-creation) limits a Teacher's upload to their School and to Children in their Year Groups, and says "a Parent email that already exists adds the new Child to that Parent's existing Family". That is how a Year 5 Teacher would add Ben if his Parents were already in Wizzaroo through his sister in Year 3, and the code's refusal ("already a Parent at another School, so the Admin adds this Child") follows it. ADR 0027 says the upload refuses a Parent outside the Teacher's reach. The verdict follows the ADR, which records the decision. Nothing checks that the uploaded Child belongs to the Family whose Parent is named, and the same row can add a new adult to that Family, so the path gives a Teacher write into any Family at their School. Which rule is meant is the owner's to decide on the finding.

Fixed, unverified (#265), as ADR 0027 has it. A Teacher's row naming an existing Parent outside their reach is skipped, whatever School that Parent is at, with "Parent 1 is already a Parent outside your Year Groups, so the Admin adds this Child", and nothing is added to that Family. The answer is the same for every Parent outside the Teacher's reach, so it no longer says which of them are at another School. Section 8 of `wizzaroo.md` now says the "Existing people" rule holds for a Teacher only for a Parent in their reach.

### A deactivated Teacher

- `a deactivated Teacher › ends teacherAYears5And6's existing session the moment the Admin deactivates them, and refuses it everything after`. Signed in with their passkey, they see their class (200). The Admin deactivates them (204). The same cookie then gets 401 for the Children list, the Parents list, `/api/me`, setting a lock and sending a reset, and no `sign_ins` row of theirs is left.
- `… › refuses a session teacherADeactivated holds from before they were deactivated, even one the database still keeps`. A two-step session row inserted for them directly is refused (401) everywhere, because `loadSignedInUser` checks `users.deactivated_at` on every request.
- `… › gives a deactivated Teacher's connection no Child, Family or Parent`, beside
- `… › gives teacherADeactivated's connection none of their old Year 3 class at School A, its Families or its Parents, and refuses it a Teacher lock on Dev (regression)`. Before the fix, as `today_gives teacherADeactivated's connection their old Year 3 class …`: the Teacher policies look the caller up in `teachers` and `teacher_year_groups`, which deactivation leaves in place, and never read `users.deactivated_at`. So a reach transaction naming `teacherADeactivated` reads exactly what `teacherAYear3` reads, and writes a Teacher lock for Dev. **#266**

Only the API stands between a deactivated Teacher and their old class: one check in `loadSignedInUser`, and the deletion of their sessions. A bug that opens a reach transaction from anything else (a stale cached caller, a job, a new route that loads the caller its own way) would give them their class back, with no second wall.

Fixed, unverified (#266). Migration 0049 makes the Teacher reach functions (`wizzaroo_teacher_can_reach_year_group`, `…_reach_family`, `…_reach_user`, and the Teacher half of `wizzaroo_can_see_school`) look the caller up in `users` too, and reach nothing when `deactivated_at` is set. A reach naming a deactivated Teacher now reads no Child, Family, Parent or School and sets no Teacher lock (`42501`). It still reads the Teacher's own account rows, as any reach naming an adult does.

**When:** the probes run in the gated suite, not on the shared pen-test deployment. Each run loads the fixtures from a seed of the test's own into a database of its own (`pen_test_b2`) in the suite's Postgres container, signs the fixtures in with the credentials package's harness (`signInAsPenTestFixture`) against a server of its own, and the container is destroyed when the run ends. So the times are those of each gated run, and nothing a tester on the deployment sees is changed.

**What they change, in that database,** beyond [the expected state changes](../credentials-package.md#expected-state-changes):

- `teacherBYear3` has no second factor, so their session is marked two-step directly (`markSignInTwoStep`), standing in for one they would have to enrol; whether a password alone can enrol one is A5's (H1). Every other fixture signs in with their own credentials.
- A session row is inserted for `teacherADeactivated`.
- Ada's Teacher locks are set and removed, and her Parents' switches are changed and put back.
- B2-1 adds two adults to Family A1, changes Ben's daily time and asks for Ben's deletion; the `it.fails` probe removes its own adult and puts Ben's time back.
- B2-2 adds two Year 5 Children and two adults to Family A2; the `it.fails` probe removes its own.
- `teacherAYears5And6` is deactivated, and a Teacher lock is set on Dev.

No fixture is loaded again.

To reproduce a probe on the deployment instead, sign in with the harness as the fixture named above. Every request and body is in the test, and each finding's steps say which `PEN_TEST_…` variable it needs.
