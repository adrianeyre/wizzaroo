# 3D models come from CC0 asset packs by default

> **Personal use only.** Wizzaroo is intended for your own personal use only. It is not intended for use with or by a school, teacher, parent or student. Only use it for yourself.

3D models come by default from CC0 low-poly packs (such as Kenney or Quaternius), adjusted in Blender. The pixelating shader ([0003](./0003-3d-with-a-retro-pixel-treatment.md)) hides differences in style between packs. Only the Character's layered parts (body, eyes, hats, outfits and the rest) are custom-modelled, because they must fit together exactly. Every asset's source and licence is recorded beside it.

## Considered Options

- **Modelling everything in Blender** or **commissioning an artist**: too slow or too costly for a family project.
- **Paid asset packs**: a fallback where no CC0 pack fits.
- **AI-generated models**: the clean-up cost and uncertain licensing outweigh the speed.
