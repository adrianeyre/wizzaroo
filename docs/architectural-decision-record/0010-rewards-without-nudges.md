# Rewards delight without nudging: no streaks and no expiring rewards

> **Personal use only.** Wizzaroo is intended for your own personal use only. It is not intended for use with or by a school, teacher, parent or student. Only use it for yourself.

The ICO Children's Code names streaks, daily login rewards and fear-of-missing-out timers as nudge techniques, and Wizzaroo commits to no features that push children to stay longer. So:

- **Bonus rounds** are surprises inside a Mission.
- The **daily reward** is a "welcome back" gift with no streak and nothing lost for missing a day.
- **Seasonal Items** come back every year rather than disappearing for good.
- There are no head-to-head leaderboards.

Stars come from effort, not from playing more: a fixed amount for each Activity finished, a one-off bonus when a Skill is Mastered, and Activities on Mastered Skills earn Stars only when they are a scheduled Review.
